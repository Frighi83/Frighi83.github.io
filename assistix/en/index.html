Assistix – Service Reports

App · For technicians and service

Screenshots

Assistix is the simple field service app for technicians: customer requests, work orders, job sheets, signatures and PDF service reports, all in one place. No account, no subscription: your data stays on your phone.

Made for maintenance and repair technicians: HVAC and boiler technicians, electricians, plumbers, IT support, solar installers, alarm and gate technicians, appliance repair and maintenance pros.

EMAILS THAT BECOME TICKETS

  • Connect your work mailbox: new customer emails arrive as requests to sort
  • Create a ticket, add to an existing one or discard with one tap
  • Signatures, antivirus banners and forwarded-message headers removed automatically
  • Reply to customers with ready-made quick replies

AT THE CUSTOMER

  • Start and stop the work timer: hours are saved automatically
  • Parts from your own price list, with quantities and prices
  • Checklists ready for each trade (boiler service, air conditioning, electrical…)
  • Before and after photos
  • Customer signature on the phone and a professional PDF service report (job sheet), numbered automatically
  • Send the report via WhatsApp, email or any app

LABOUR AND BILLING

  • Hourly rate, call-out fee and mileage: totals worked out for you
  • Special rate per customer, warranty/free jobs, hidden prices for single parts
  • "To bill" list and Excel export for your accountant

CUSTOMERS, EQUIPMENT AND SCHEDULE

  • Import customers from your phone contacts
  • Equipment with brand, model, serial number and full job history
  • Service due dates with reminders
  • Day and week schedule, with a WhatsApp message to confirm the appointment

AND MORE

  • Two ways of working: jobs and reports only, or full tickets
  • Built-in user guide
  • Backup to a single file (with optional password) you can save on Google Drive
  • Light and dark theme, Italian and English

Launch offer: all features are free. The app shows a small banner at the bottom of lists, never during your work.
