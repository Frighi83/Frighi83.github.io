Blockflare: Block Puzzle Game

Screenshots

Blockflare is a free block puzzle, relaxing and challenging, with animated backgrounds and lots of game modes. Drag the colourful blocks onto the grid, complete rows and columns to make them burst and score as high as you can!

Easy to learn, hard to put down: the perfect block game for a quick break or a long session. Works offline too.

✨ WHAT'S NEW

  • Animated themed backgrounds: lake sunset, neon city, crystal cave, sea bed, autumn forest and dawn
  • Reserve: keep a piece aside to use at the right moment
  • Spectacular combos: the more lines you clear in one move, the more sparks and points you get
  • Quick tutorial the first time you play
  • Glossy blocks and completely refreshed graphics

🎮 6 GAME MODES

  • Daily challenge: the same grid for everyone, a new challenge every day
  • Marathon: play as long as there is space and beat your record
  • Puzzle: levels with goals to reach in just a few moves
  • Time attack: the faster you are, the more points you score
  • Rise: the grid rises from the bottom, hold on as long as you can
  • Zen: no rush, just relax

💥 POWERFUL HELPERS

Hammer, rocket and bomb get you out of trouble when space runs out. Earn coins by playing, with the daily bonus or by watching a short video, and spend them on helpers.

🎯 DAILY GOALS

Three new goals every day with coin rewards: the more you play, the more you earn.

📊 STATISTICS

Check your records, games played, lines cleared and playing time.

🎨 COLOURFUL THEMES

Unlock new block colours with coins: Classic, Neon, Pastel, Ocean, Sunset.

🧠 TRAIN YOUR BRAIN

A logic and strategy puzzle: plan every move, build combos and line streaks for huge scores.

✨ MORE FEATURES

  • Free, plays without a connection
  • Effects, sounds and vibration (can be turned off)
  • Daily bonus with rewards
  • Available in English and Italian
  • Privacy options always available in Settings
