Flicker: Firefly Cave Flight

Screenshots

Flicker is a one-tap flying arcade game with a twist: your light is your life. Guide a cute firefly through hand-painted dark caves, dodge stalactites and stalagmites, and catch drops of light before your glow fades out. An endless flyer that is simple to learn, hard to master and perfect for quick runs of a few minutes.

✨ ONE-TAP CONTROLS

  • Tap the screen to fly — no complicated controls
  • Catch light drops to keep your firefly glowing
  • Your light fades every second: run out and the game is over
  • A red glow and a heartbeat warn you when your light is almost gone

💥 GLOW BLAST POWER-UP

Catch 5 light drops in a row to charge the Glow Blast: a burst of light that smashes every rock on screen. Use it with a button or a second finger without breaking your flying rhythm.

❤️ HEART OF LIGHT

Grab the rare Heart of Light to refill your glow completely and escape the darkness.

🌍 4 WORLDS TO EXPLORE

  • Crystal Cave, full of shining crystals
  • Deep Abyss, an underwater world of corals, anemones and bubbles
  • Night Forest under the moonlight
  • Glacier beneath the northern lights

Each hand-painted world has its own obstacles, atmosphere and original music.

🔥 A CHALLENGE THAT GROWS WITH YOU

Moving rocks, snapping jaws, tight zig-zags and showers of light: the further you fly, the more alive the cave becomes. Beat your high score and see how far your light can go.

🎁 REASONS TO COME BACK EVERY DAY

  • Daily gift that grows when you play several days in a row
  • Missions with drop rewards
  • 6 unique firefly characters: the Classic one plus Azure, Rose, Violet, Golden and Rainbow to unlock
  • Try any world or firefly before unlocking it
  • Your top 10 best scores
  • Share your record with friends

🎮 WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT

  • Casual arcade game for quick breaks
  • Beautiful hand-painted art, glowing effects and relaxing music
  • Short tutorial for new players and a full "How to play" page
  • Play offline, no account needed
  • Available in English and Italian
