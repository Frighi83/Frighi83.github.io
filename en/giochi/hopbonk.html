Hop Bonk: Pixel Platformer

Screenshots

Jump on your enemies' heads and use every stomp to bounce even higher!

Hop Bonk is a fast, satisfying pixel art platformer where every jump counts. Bounce from enemy to enemy, chain combos without touching the ground, unlock the double jump and the dash, and dodge spinning saws and sharp spikes.

★ 36 HANDCRAFTED LEVELS IN 3 WORLDS

From your first stomps to the final summit, difficulty rises step by step. Each world has its own colours and new surprises: springs, thin ledges, crumbling blocks and moving platforms.

★ 3 STARS IN EVERY LEVEL

Finish the level, finish it without dying, beat the target time. 108 stars to collect!

★ 3 LIVES PER LEVEL

No endless retries: every jump really matters. Lost all your lives? Watch a short video to continue, or start the level again.

★ SIMPLE, PRECISE TOUCH CONTROLS

Move with your left thumb, jump with your right. Hold longer to jump higher. Left-handed mode included.

★ DAILY CHALLENGE, 21 ACHIEVEMENTS AND A SKIN SHOP

A new goal every day. Earn coins and dress up your hero with colourful and animated skins.

★ PLAY ANYWHERE

Works offline. Controller support and adjustable vibration.

Free to play, with ads.

Based on the open-source game "Stomper" by Diego Escalante (GitHub Game Off 2022).
